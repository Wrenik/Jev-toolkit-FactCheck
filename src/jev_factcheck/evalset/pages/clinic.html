Zelvorin phase 3 results

In the phase 3 trial of Zelvorin, 412 adults took part. Symptoms fell by 23% compared with placebo after twelve weeks.

Headache was the most common side effect and affected 9% of participants. The study was not designed to assess long-term outcomes. Zelvorin is not approved for use in children.
